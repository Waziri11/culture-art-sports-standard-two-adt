How to play penalty kick:

1. Download the GCompris App on your smartphone, tablet or computer;
2. Choose the penalty kick game;
3. Observe the goal and where the goalkeeper is positioned;
4. Choose the side you want to direct the ball to avoid the goalkeeper catching it;
5. Double-click or touch quickly twice to kick the ball; and
6. If the goalkeeper catches the ball, click the ball once to return.
