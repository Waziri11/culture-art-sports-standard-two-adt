Fishing

Fishing is an activity that involves catching fish. It is usually done in lakes, rivers, dams and oceans. Some of the tools used in fishing include a hook, bait, net and a canoe. Varieties of fish caught include tilapia, catfish, nile perch, octopus, trout and sardiness. They are used for food and business purposes. Wanyasa, Wapemba, Wadigo, Wajita and Waha are some of the communities that engage in fishing activities.
