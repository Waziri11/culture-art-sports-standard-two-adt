Respecting religious differences
and ethics
I recognise and appreciate the beliefs of other people.
Read the passage and answer the questions that follows:

People go to pray so that they become good and kind with acceptable ethics. There are different religions like Islam, Christianity and Hinduism in our community. Praying helps people to be kind, loving, respectful, patient and work together. It also, helps them to avoid bad behaviour such as being greedy, stealing, fighting and being unfaithful. When people pray, they learn to be good and behave nicely. Good behaviour is important for a person and his or her community development. A person with good ethics is accepted in the community.
