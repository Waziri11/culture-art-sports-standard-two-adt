Activity 1

Write down what you learn when you go to places of prayer.

Read the story and answer the questions that follows:

At Amani Primary School there were three pupils named Furaha, Tumaini and Baraka. These pupils were in Standard Two and had different beliefs. They attended different sessions in school, including religious. In the religious session they learn to be loving, patient, cooperative and care for others. Furaha and Tumaini were obedient and eager to attend all sessions. Baraka did not like to participate in religious sessions. During the session, he would rather run away and hide behind the classroom. Because of this behaviour, Baraka was naughty, selfish and did not care about others. One day, the teacher told them the effects of not attending religious sessions. The teacher said, “If you miss the religious sessions you cannot learn good things for the community. Therefore, you will not be a well-behaved child and not accepted by the community”. Furaha and Tumaini decided to talk to Baraka.
