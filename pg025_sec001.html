Kabula:
I’m fine, Mzee Masanja. Where are you going, it is very early in the morning.
Mzee Masanja:
I’m going to the Mosque to pray.
Kabula:
Okay Mzee Masanja! Can I ask you a question?
Mzee Masanja:
Of course, you may ask, Kabula.
Kabula:
I usually hear the sound coming from the Mosque early in the morning, What does that sound mean?
Mzee Masanja:
That’s a good question, Kabula. That sound is called Adhana. For us Muslims, when we hear the Adhana, we prepare and go to the Mosque to pray.
Kabula:
Oh! So, the Adhana is like the bell that rings at the church?
Mzee Masanja:
Yes, Kabula. In Churches, they use a bell, and in the Mosque, we use the Adhana to remind people that it is time for prayers.
