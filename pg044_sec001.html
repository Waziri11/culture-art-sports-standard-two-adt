Rabbit and the mirror

One day Rabbit went to the shop and bought a mirror. When standing in front of it, he saw the image. Everything he does, the image imitates. Rabbit got angry and said to the image, “Why do you imitate everything I do?” He got scared and decided to return the mirror to the shop.

Hyena and the shadow

Hyena used to walk alone wherever he went. One day, it was very sunny. As he walked, he saw a shadow of someone following him. He tried to run, but the shadow ran after him wherever he went. When he sat down, the shadow sat down too. Finally, he got tired and decided to sit under a tree. Hyena was surprised to see that the shadow had gone. Hyena returned to the sun and saw the shadow following again. Hyena got scared and ran back to the tree.
