I participate in running with a stick game.
A game of running with a stick

A game of running with a stick involves two teams of two players each. The teams compete to run with a stick in their hands. Players of the same team exchange the stick in the middle of the race. Then, the last receiver runs to the finishing line.

How to play:
1.
Prepare the body;
(a) Walk quickly and run slowly for 2 to 3 minutes;
(b) Stand and jump for 10 to 15 times; and
(c) Stretch your body parts for 2 to 3 minutes.
2. Form teams of two players and each team must have one stick;
