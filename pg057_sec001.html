The Target game

The target game involves the use of hands and feet to target objects. Players use a ball to hit the target by rolling, throwing, kicking and bouncing.

How to play

Part one (by using hands):

1.

Prepare the body by running slowly for 2 to 3 minutes, then rotate and swing your arms;

2.

Choose a safe and open flat area;

3.

Mark the position where you will stand, then arrange three bottles filled with sand or water at a distance of 10 to 20 steps or 3 meters to 5 meters;
