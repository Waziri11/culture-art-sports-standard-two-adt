Comedy

Comedy is a type of entertainment intended to make people laugh. It involves telling funny stories and jokes to the audience.

Read the following comedy, then answer the questions:

Kibo and Mawenzi

Mawenzi was crying. On the way met Kibo. Kibo asked, “Hello! Friend, why are you crying?” Mawenzi replied, “I had two one thousand shillings, one to buy flour and the other to buy vegetables. Now, I have mixed them and I don’t know which one is for flour and which one is for vegetables.”
