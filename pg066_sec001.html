Part two (by using feet):

  1. 1.

    Prepare your body by running slowly for 2 to 3 minutes, then stretch your body;

  2. 2.

    Use a flat area to draw the H shape as shown in the illustration in part one of the game;

  3. 3.

    Form two teams of six players each;

  4. 4.

    Five players for each team will stay inside the defending zone, and one will play behind the opposing team in the search zone;

  5. 5.

    Team members are not allowed to enter the free zone or search zone. Only the searcher is allowed to stay in the search zone outside the team’s zone;

  6. 6.

    Each team will pass and dribble the ball with feet to find a space to pass it to their searcher to score points;

  7. 7.

    Each team also has the responsibility to prevent the opposing team from passing the ball through into their zone; and

  8. 8.

    If the searcher receives the ball without involving hands, they will return to the team, and the one who passed the ball will become the searcher.
