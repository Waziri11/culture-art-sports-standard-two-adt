Farming

Farming is an activity that involves the production of food and cash crops. Farmers grow crops like maize, rice, beans, bananas, potatoes, cashewnuts, sisal, palm trees and cotton. Some of the communities that are involved in farming include Wahehe, Wanyakyusa, Wanyamwezi, Wahaya, Wazigua, Wasukuma, Wapogoro, Wayao and Waha.
