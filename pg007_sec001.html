Our culture
I recognise the traditional houses of Tanzania.

Traditional houses

A house is a building where people live in. Traditional houses are the type of houses that are used by the Tanzanian community as their home.

Types of traditional houses

Tanzania has different types of traditional houses such as msonge, banda and tembe.

Msonge house

Msonge house is a type of house built with a pointed top. They are built by using poles, banana leaves, tree bark, mud, plant branches or tough grass. Some Tanzanian communities that use these houses include the Wahaya, Wasafwa, wafipa, Wachaga, Waha and Wanyambo.
