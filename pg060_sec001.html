Part two (by using feet)

1.

Prepare the body by running slowly for 2 to 3 minutes, then jump many times and stretch the body parts;

2.

Choose a safe and open flat area;

3.

Arrange two bottles with a space in between for the ball to pass;

4.

Place a kicking point mark of 10 to 20 steps or 3 meters to 5 meters from where the bottles are placed;

Arranged bottles in the pitch

5.

Place the ball at the kicking point on the mark;

6.

Kick the ball and pass it between the bottles without knocking them down;
