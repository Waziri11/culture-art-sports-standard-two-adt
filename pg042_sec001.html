Heroic recitation

Heroic recitation is a dramatic and expressive way of performing heroic actions. It can be used to entertain and educate people in the community.

Read the following recitation then answer the questions:
Moo! Moo! Moo! I am a cow! I give you milk and meat. Eat meat, drink milk to build your health. Moo! Moo! Moo!
Ha! Ha! Ha! I am the tallest of all animals, with attractive neck. My swagger walk attract local and foreign tourists, to raise the economy. Ha! Ha! Ha!
Ohi! Ohi! Ohi! I am the most attractive with ordered colours. Use my skin for different items. Ohi! Ohi! Ohi!
