Introduction

The book of Culture, Art and Sports has been prepared per the Curriculum and Syllabus for Primary Education of 2023 issued by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology. This book is the Second Edition, improved from the Sports and Art Book published in 2018 in line with the curriculum of 2016. This book aims to enable the pupil to develop competency in culture, arts and sports through various learning activities in the following areas:

  • 1. Appreciate one’s culture and that of other people;
  • 2. Demonstrate religious tolerance;
  • 3. Demonstrate ethical practice;
  • 4. Creative works of art; and
  • 5. Participate in various sports and games.
